Our Philosophy

Slow dough.
Raging fire.

Every pizza at Fuoco begins 48 hours before you taste it. Our dough cold-ferments for two days, developing the complex flavour that fast food can never replicate. We use Tipo 00 flour imported from Mulino Caputo in Naples — the same mill that has supplied pizzerias for over a century.

We cook at 900°F in a hand-built stone oven fuelled by sustainably sourced oak wood. Ninety seconds. That's all it takes — when the heat is honest and the ingredients are real.

🪵
