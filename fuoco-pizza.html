Our Philosophy

Slow dough.
Raging fire.
No shortcuts.

Every pizza at Fuoco begins 48 hours before you taste it. Our dough cold-ferments for two days, developing the complex flavour that fast food can never replicate.

We cook at 900°F in a hand-built stone oven imported from Naples. Ninety seconds. That's all it takes — when the heat is honest and the ingredients are real.
